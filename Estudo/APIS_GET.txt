5º Obter as variveis groups atraves do Project ID: https://vmsys-tfs01/DefaultCollection/{Project}/_apis/distributedtask/variablegroups

6º Obter os Environments atraves do project: https://vmsys-tfs01/DefaultCollection/{Project}/_apis/distributedtask/environments

7º Obter a conexão entre Environment e pipeline: https://vmsys-tfs01/DefaultCollection/{Project}/_apis/pipelines/pipelinepermissions/environment/{environment}
